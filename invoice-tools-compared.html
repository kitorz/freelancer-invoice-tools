Wave vs Zoho Invoice vs Invoice Ninja: which free plan actually chases late payers?

All three send invoices for free. Only one of them reminds your client for free — and none of them writes the firm email for you. If you are looking for a Wave alternative because the free plan leaves the chasing to you, the table below is the whole answer, plus a fourth option: a $29 one-time kit that does the chasing part, no account needed.

The verdict, up front

  • Want free automatic reminders? Zoho Invoice is the only one of the three that includes them on its free plan.
  • Want unlimited free invoices with no client cap? Wave's free plan has no invoice limit — but automatic reminders cost $19 a month.
  • Have five or fewer clients? Invoice Ninja's free plan is the most feature-rich — but automatic reminders cost $14 a month.

Wave

Wave is an invoicing tool that comes with a general ledger. The free Starter plan is the real product, not a teaser: unlimited invoices, estimates, bills and bookkeeping records, plus invoicing from the mobile app. It is built for businesses in the United States and Canada.

Watch out: automatic late-payment reminders are a Pro feature ($19 a month), so on the free plan every reminder is one you write and send yourself. Free invoices carry Wave branding unless you pay for Pro, and every card payment carries a per-transaction fee.

Zoho Invoice

Zoho Invoice is free invoicing software for small businesses, with no credit card and no ads. The free plan covers quotes, invoices, recurring invoices, expenses, time tracking and a client portal — and it is the only one of the three that sends automatic payment reminders without a paid tier.

Watch out: the caps are the thing to plan around — 500 invoices a year, 2 users, 3 projects — and free invoices carry "Powered by Zoho Invoice" branding. Invoices go out in your base currency only. Zoho deletes the data of accounts inactive for more than 180 days, after a notice, so log in now and then or keep a backup.

Invoice Ninja

Invoice Ninja's hosted free plan covers up to 5 clients with unlimited invoicing, recurring invoices, online payments, a client portal, and time and expense tracking. It is also open source, so it can be self-hosted instead.

Watch out: a sixth client means Pro at $14 a month, and automatic reminders are a Pro feature too — on the free plan, every overdue invoice is a reminder you write and send yourself. Free invoices carry "Created by Invoice Ninja" branding.
