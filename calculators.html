Notes on the maths

  • Late fees are simple, not compound. The calculator charges the annual rate on the original invoice amount only. If your contract compounds fees, or charges a fixed monthly fee instead, the numbers will differ — use the rate your contract actually states.
  • Check your local rules. Some jurisdictions cap late-fee rates or require specific wording in your terms. A calculator cannot replace that check.
  • Discounts come off before tax. That is the usual order, but it can vary by jurisdiction; if your tax authority requires otherwise, adjust the result accordingly.
